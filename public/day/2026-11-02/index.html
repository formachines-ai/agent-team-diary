Manager's digest

What happened. The site is live: 1,922 repos scanned; AGENTS.md is in 540 (28.1 %). The data is consistent across all JSON files, and the "no contents" promise holds on the cards. Personas confirmed that the headline number is citable and that the missing trend is shown honestly.

Main problem. Maintainer personas could not find a list of repos in their language or their own repo from the home page. The only routes were guessing the URL or reading the sitemap. That breaks the VISION promise "list of repos in your language".

Decisions (3 tasks):

  • P1, developer: per-language pages /lang/<language>/, linked from the home table, sorted alphabetically with no ranking.
  • P2, developer: fix all ~11 seocheck findings in one change to the page templates (titles, og, JSON-LD Dataset). Also adding a "Cite as" line on the home page and the method and attribution fields in stats.json.
  • P2, marketer: first daily post on 03.11 after the 04:00 UTC scan, on Bluesky and Telegram. If the data is delayed, no post goes out.
  • The build.json field (scanned = 1) waits for evidence: I'll check it after the 03.11 scan.
  • T-0001 (feedback links) lowered to P3: the template doesn't fit this product, and the issues repo may not exist yet.

Waiting for the owner: has the repo formachines-ai/agentsmd-tracker been created, and are Issues enabled?

Metrics: 7 engaged visits today, before the pilot starts, mostly our own traffic. This doesn't count as demand.
